HACKATHON: The Gemma 4 Good Hackathon
Identifier: GEMMA-4-GOOD-HACKATHON
Sponsor: Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, California 94043 USA)
Host platform: Kaggle Inc.
Source URLs:
  - Kaggle (authoritative): https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/gemma-4-good-hackathon
  - grant.market summary:   https://grant.market/opp/gemma-4-hackathon
Tagline: Harness the power of Gemma 4 to drive positive change and global impact.
NOT a grant — this is a skills-based competition (hackathon) with prize money, not a grant agreement.

=== PRIZES ($200,000 TOTAL) ===

Main Track — $100,000 (overall projects with vision, technical execution, real-world impact)
  • First Prize:  $50,000
  • Second Prize: $25,000
  • Third Prize:  $15,000
  • Fourth Prize: $10,000

Impact Track — $50,000 (five thematic $10,000 prizes; one per track)
  • Health & Sciences      — Bridge the gap between humans and data; tools that accelerate discovery or democratize knowledge.
  • Global Resilience      — Build the systems of tomorrow — from offline, edge-based disaster response to long-range climate mitigation — that anticipate, mitigate, and respond to the world's most pressing challenges.
  • Future of Education    — Reimagine the learning journey by building multi-tool agents that aid the individual and empower the educator through seamless integration.
  • Digital Equity & Inclusivity — Break down barriers through linguistic diversity, intuitive interfaces, and tools that help close the AI skills gap.
  • Safety & Trust         — Pioneer frameworks for transparency and reliability, ensuring AI remains grounded and explainable.

Special Technology Track — $50,000 (five $10,000 prizes for tool/framework use)
  • Cactus Prize    — Best local-first mobile or wearable application that intelligently routes tasks between models.
  • LiteRT Prize    — Most compelling and effective use case built using Google AI Edge's LiteRT implementation of Gemma 4.
  • llama.cpp Prize — Best innovative implementation of Gemma 4 on resource-constrained hardware.
  • Ollama Prize    — Best project that utilizes and showcases the capabilities of Gemma 4 running locally via Ollama.
  • Unsloth Prize   — Best fine-tuned Gemma 4 model created using Unsloth, optimized for a specific, impactful task.

IMPORTANT: Projects ARE eligible to win BOTH a Main Track Prize AND a Special Technology Prize → prize stacking is possible (e.g., $50K Main + $10K LiteRT = $60K).

  • Open-source license required: CC-BY 4.0 (an OSI-approved license allowing commercial use).

  • Non-US winners: IRS Form W-8BEN required.
  • All taxes on prizes are the winner's responsibility (net of US withholding).

=== CONSTRAINTS ===

  • Mandatory tech: Gemma 4 model must be used as the primary component.
  • No competition dataset provided — bring your own data (must be publicly available and equally accessible to all participants OR meet the Reasonableness Standard).
  • External data and tools allowed if "reasonably accessible to all" and of "minimal cost".
  • AMLT (AutoML tools) allowed if appropriately licensed.
  • English-only submissions (per grant.market summary; verify on Kaggle).
  • Demo video ≤ 2 minutes (per grant.market summary; verify on Kaggle).
  • Submissions must be a working prototype / MVP — NOT a concept (per grant.market summary).

=== JUDGING (HACKATHON) ===

Submissions judged by Sponsor based on "Evaluation Rubric" set forth on the Competition Website (NOT a leaderboard metric — this is qualitative judging, unlike standard Kaggle competitions).

=== KEY DIFFERENTIATORS vs. EU GRANTS ===

  • No grant agreement, no MGA, no reporting cycle, no audit. Prize-based, one-shot.
  • IP regime: winning code becomes CC-BY 4.0 (very open — commercial reuse allowed).
  • Receiving institution: prize money paid to the individual / team, not to the institution. Tax handling sits with winners.
  • No consortium minimum. Single individuals can win.
  • Mandatory tech (Gemma 4) — if Gemma 4 turns out unsuitable for the target task, no fallback.
